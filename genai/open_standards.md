### Open Standards vs. Vendor-Specific Alternatives

| Standard / Specification | Domain Focus | Primary Telemetry / Metadata Type | Popular Vendor-Specific Alternatives (Proprietary / Commercial) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **[OpenTelemetry](https://opentelemetry.io)** | Cloud-native applications & Microservices | Traces, Metrics, Logs | **Datadog Agent**, **Dynatrace OneAgent**, **New Relic APM Agent**, **Splunk Forwarder** |
| **[OpenLineage](https://openlineage.io)** | Data Engineering & Pipelines | Dataset dependencies & Job runs | **Monte Carlo**, **Databand** (IBM), **Acceldata**, **Dynatrace Data Observability** |
| **[Apache Ossie](https://apache.org)** | Analytics, BI, & AI Agents | Semantic models, Business metrics, & Ontologies | **Looker LookML** (Google Cloud), **Cube**, **dbt Semantic Layer** (dbt Labs) |
| **[OpenMetadata](https://open-metadata.org)** | Data Governance | Data Catalogs, Schemas, & Ownership | **Collibra**, **Alation**, **Atlan**, **Informatica IDMC**, **Microsoft Purview** |
