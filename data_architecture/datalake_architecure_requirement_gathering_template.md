| **Data Architecture**   | Data Architecture                         | * Multi-tenant Data Lake (aka Data Mesh). Each provider publishes their domain-specific datasets to their respective Data Lake; to avoid duplicates, only datasets identified as the Source of Truth are published. Consumers discover and self-serve data through governed subscriptions (standardized interfaces). <br> * Data Lake-first approach: data is first published to the Data Lake and then consumed from it — even for team-level reporting. Promotes a “Data as a Product” mindset. <br> * Federated ownership and governance through shared (central) platform and standards. <br> * Decoupled storage and compute: enables independent scaling, cost efficiency, and flexibility in choosing compute engines. <br> * Support for multi-mode data ingestion: batch, streaming, and near real-time (Lambda Data Architecture) to serve a wide range of analytical and operational use cases. <br> * Tools and technology flexibility: allows teams to choose fit-for-purpose tools and technologies for data ingestion and processing. |                                                                         |
